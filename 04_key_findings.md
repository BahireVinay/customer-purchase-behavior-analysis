# Key Findings

## Executive Summary

This project analyzes 2,200 synthetic customer transactions across 250 customers during 2025. The analysis focuses on purchasing frequency, customer value, category performance, geographic behavior and acquisition channels.

## Key Findings

1. **Revenue leader:** Home & Kitchen generated the highest net revenue at ₹2,796,999.95.
2. **Top product:** Air Fryer generated ₹1,044,725.85 in net revenue.
3. **Strongest city:** Aurangabad generated ₹1,017,681.10 in net revenue.
4. **Best acquisition channel by revenue:** Mobile App generated ₹1,834,192.30.
5. **Repeat customer rate:** 99.6% of customers made more than one purchase.
6. **Top customer:** CUST0097 generated ₹73,181.70 across 11 transactions.
7. **Average transaction value:** ₹3,099.28.
8. **Total net revenue:** ₹6,818,420.75.

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritize retention campaigns for repeat customers and high-value customer segments.
- Use category-level revenue trends to guide inventory and promotional planning.
- Investigate high-performing cities for localized offers and customer acquisition.
- Compare acquisition channels using both revenue and average transaction value rather than transaction volume alone.
- Create targeted cross-sell campaigns around customers' most frequently purchased categories.

## Portfolio Notes

The dataset is synthetic. The project demonstrates the workflow expected in an entry-level Data Analyst role:

**Raw transaction data → validation → SQL aggregation → customer-level analysis → Excel reporting → business recommendations**
